## The entry

> By continuing, the user authorizes the service to retain one emotionally significant spoon for quality assurance.

**Character count: 113** (one sentence, English, within the 1–200 character limit).

## The joke

Buried in the fine print of a data-retention clause, the service doesn't just keep your data — it keeps one emotionally significant spoon. Not your data, not your metadata: a spoon. And not just any spoon — the one that matters to you. It's retained, of course, strictly for quality assurance.

## Run it
